Help

Everything a new user needs — about 2 minutes to read.

What BroClips does

You record a video where someone talks — a game with your commentary, a lesson, a programming tutorial, a podcast. BroClips edits it for you:

Shorts

For YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels: the best moments, standing up (9:16), with big captions word by word.

A clean long video

For YouTube: the whole recording with the long silences cut out (“jump cuts”).

Titles and thumbnails

Titles, descriptions and hashtags in your language, and thumbnails you can change with clicks.

Your video files are only read. BroClips never changes, moves or deletes them.

First steps — choose your AI

Do this once, in Settings.

BroClips needs two AIs: a Thinking AI (it picks the moments and writes titles) and a Listening AI (it turns speech into text). Pick one of these two ways in Settings:

Option A — free and private

Everything stays on your PC.

  1. Install Ollama from ollama.com (Download → Windows) and start it.
  2. Download a model: press the Windows key, type cmd, press Enter, then type ollama pull gemma3:12b and press Enter. It downloads about 8 GB once. On a PC with a small graphics card (or none) use ollama pull gemma3:4b instead, and write gemma3:4b as the model in Settings.
  3. In Settings keep Thinking AI: Ollama and Listening AI: On this PC, then press Test on both.

“On this PC” listening needs the local speech part of the installer (answer Y to “Local speech-to-text”). The first time it downloads its speech model (about 3 GB for the best one).

Option B — paste a key

A cloud AI does the work.

  • Google Gemini (has a free plan): get a key at aistudio.google.com/apikey.
  • OpenAI (paid): platform.openai.com/api-keys. OpenRouter, Groq and LM Studio work too (same section, pick the service).
  • Anthropic Claude (paid): console.anthropic.com → API keys.

Paste the key in Settings, press Save, then Test. You can mix: for example local listening + a Gemini key for thinking.

What is sent with a key: the text of what is said goes to the Thinking AI's company. If you choose a cloud Listening AI, your audio goes to it too. Pictures from the video are sent only if you switch on “Allow sending pictures” in Settings. Your keys stay on this PC (in secrets.json) and each key is only sent to its own company.

How to make clips

Five steps. The first project takes a minute to set up.
1New project

Press New project (bottom left) and give it a name, for example “Python lessons”.

2Describe the videos

In the big box: what they are, who watches, which platforms, which language, what tone.

3Pick the details

The video type, the language and where you post. Switch on 🏃 Marathon mode if you want the AI to think longer for better moments.

4Add videos

Press Add videos and choose your recordings (or paste their paths).

5Press Make

Its progress shows at the top and on the video's page. You can close the window — BroClips keeps working.

The work line

BroClips does one job at a time, in order, so your PC does not get stuck. The pill at the top of every page shows what it is doing.

In Settings you can list programs (for example a game) — while one of them is open, BroClips waits. If you want it to work anyway, press Run now. If you close BroClips in the middle of a job, it continues when you start it again.

Where are my files?

  • Your videos stay exactly where they were.
  • Everything BroClips makes is in its data folder (normally C:\Users\you\BroClips) → projects\<project>\videos\<video>\: the Shorts, the long video, thumbnails and texts. Each video page has an Open folder button.
  • Settings and keys: settings.json and secrets.json in the data folder. Problems are written to the logs folder.

Questions

I have no NVIDIA graphics card. Does it work?
Yes, just slower. In Settings choose the video encoder “Processor only” and the listening model “small”. Or use a key: then the cloud does the heavy thinking.
It is very slow.
The first run downloads models, so it is the slowest. A local AI on a PC without a strong graphics card is slow — choose smaller models (gemma3:4b, listening “small”) or use a key. Marathon mode is slower on purpose.
Which model should I use?
Thinking: gemma3:12b with a graphics card of 12 GB or more, else gemma3:4b. Listening: “Automatic” picks large-v3 with an NVIDIA card and small without.
Does anything leave my PC?
With Ollama and “On this PC”: nothing. With a key: only what is written above (text, audio if cloud listening, pictures only if you allow it) goes to that company.
The Test says Ollama is not running.
Start Ollama from the Start menu (it shows a small llama icon next to the clock), then test again.
Something failed.
The message tells you what happened. More details are in the logs folder (button above). You can ask for help on GitHub: github.com/omar1001/BroClips/issues.

Closing BroClips

Closing this window does not stop BroClips: it keeps working in the background and the Desktop shortcut opens it again. To stop it completely (for example before you update it), press this button. Work that is waiting in line continues the next time you start BroClips.
